Quickstart

Install editable, generate a synthetic session, compute SQIs, and add a stub annotation.

{`python -m pip install -e . physioqc simulate run_001 --fs 30000 --channels 64 --seconds 8 physioqc sqi run_001 --win 1.0 --step 0.25 --mains-hz 60 \ --out-parquet run_001/sqi.parquet --out-events run_001/sqi_events.jsonl physioqc annotate run_001 --ts-start 2.0 --ts-end 3.5 --channels 15 \ --tag line_noise --severity moderate --notes "demo"`}
SQIs (MVP)
  • rms_uV: sqrt(mean(x²))
  • line_ratio: power @ 50/60 Hz ±Δ and harmonics ÷ total
  • flatline_pct: σ < ε or repeated samples
  • clipping_pct: % samples near ADC rails
  • hf_lf_ratio: simple HF/LF spectral energy proxy
Windowing: left‑closed, right‑open [t0, t1); step = --step.
