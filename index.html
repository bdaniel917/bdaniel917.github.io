About

Engineering for physiological data.

My background combines electrical engineering, digital signal processing, machine learning, and scientific computing. I have worked with EEG, EMG, PSG, ECG, inertial sensors, and audio across pharmaceutical research, digital health, startups, and academic labs.

I am especially interested in systems that sit close to the data: acquisition, signal quality, preprocessing, algorithm development, validation, and the infrastructure required to make analysis reproducible and usable by collaborators.

I am based in San Francisco and interested in neurotechnology, brain- computer interfaces, wearable sensing, medical devices, and applied research engineering.
